Privacy Notice

Last updated: 27 April 2026

Is It Kicking Off? ("the site", "we") is a satirical UK aggravation threat-index dashboard run by RAW (hello@inkbyte.co.uk). This notice explains what data the site touches, where it goes, and how to get in touch.

What we collect directly

Almost nothing. The site does not require sign-in, does not set advertising cookies, and does not run third-party tracking pixels. We do not have a database of users.

  • No accounts. There is nothing to register for.
  • No personal profile. We do not ask for your name, email, address, age or location.
  • No advertising cookies. No DoubleClick, no Facebook pixel, no remarketing.
  • Optional newsletter. If you choose to give us your email via the newsletter form, that address is stored only to send the newsletter, and you can unsubscribe at any time by emailing us.

Local storage on your device

The dashboard saves a small amount of data in your browser's sessionStorage and localStorage so that pages like the Snapshot view can read your most recent reading without re-fetching everything. This data lives on your device, never leaves it, and is cleared when you clear your browser storage. It contains the score, the tier label, the selected region, and a list of signal scores. No personal information.

Live data sources

The dashboard fetches live public data from a number of independent providers. When your browser makes those requests, those providers will see your IP address and standard browser headers in the same way they would for any website. We do not control or have access to those logs. Sources include (non-exhaustive):

  • BBC News, The Guardian, Sky News, Daily Mail and Mirror RSS feeds
  • Open-Meteo (weather)
  • UK Government bank-holidays API and gov.uk
  • GDELT Project (news tone)
  • data.police.uk and news.met.police.uk
  • data.elexon.co.uk and api.nationalgrideso.com (electricity grid)
  • Reddit, TVMaze, TheSportsDB, Wikipedia
  • Apple Marketing Tools (charts)
  • National Lottery (jackpot)
  • Twitter/X embed widget (only when a live tweet is rendered)

CORS proxies

Some upstream feeds block direct cross-origin browser access. Where that's the case, the dashboard routes the request through a public CORS proxy (currently api.allorigins.win and corsproxy.io). Those proxies see the URL being requested and your IP. They do not see anything personal you have typed into the dashboard, because the dashboard does not collect anything personal. If you would rather not use proxies, blocking them in your browser will simply cause the affected modules to fall back to their cached or modelled values — the rest of the page still works.

Optional history (Supabase)

If, in the future, the dashboard records its daily snapshots to a database for historical charting, that database is hosted on Supabase (EU region). Only the score, tier, region and signal scores are written. No IP addresses, no device identifiers, no personal information.

Analytics

If we add basic, privacy-respecting analytics in future (e.g. Plausible or Cloudflare Web Analytics), that will be stated here. Neither sets cookies or fingerprints visitors.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

If you live in the UK or EU you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected, and to ask for it to be deleted. Because we don't hold any personal data about you in the first place, this is usually a one-line answer — but you are welcome to ask. Email hello@inkbyte.co.uk.

Disclaimer

Last updated: 27 April 2026

This is a comedy dashboard. The "Aggravation Threat Index" is illustrative and intentionally exaggerated. It draws on real public data sources but combines them into a deliberately silly score about whether the UK is "kicking off".

Specifically:

  • The Stella Artois Index, the Kebab & Fast-Food Index and the Cocaine in UK Waters module are modelled — that is, they are estimates produced from baseline assumptions and time-of-day curves, not measurements from breweries, fast-food chains or water authorities.
  • Live news feeds (BBC, Guardian, Sky, etc.) are presented unchanged but the tension scoring applied to them is our own opinionated keyword model.
  • Sports, lottery and grid data are pulled from public sources and presented as-is, but with comedic framing.
  • The "verdict" lines and tier names are generated lines of comedy writing.

Do not use this site to make any real decision about your safety, your finances, your travel, your relationships, or whether you should "go out tonight". If you are worried about a real-world situation in your area, contact the relevant authorities or check the official gov.uk sources.

We do not encourage excessive drinking, drug use, gambling or any of the other behaviours referenced satirically on this site. If any of those topics are affecting you, support is available — for example via NHS alcohol advice and BeGambleAware.

Cookies & Storage

Last updated: 27 April 2026

This site does not set tracking cookies.

What it does use:

  • sessionStorage — for the most recent score and signal breakdown, so the Snapshot page can render without re-fetching. Cleared automatically when you close the tab.
  • localStorage — to remember your selected region (e.g. London / Manchester / National) between visits, and a small handful of UI preferences. Stored only on your device.
  • URL hash permalinks — when you share a snapshot via the Share button, the score, tier, region and date are encoded in the URL itself (after the #). No information is sent to us; your browser interprets it locally.

If/when we add a privacy-respecting analytics product (Plausible or Cloudflare Web Analytics), that will be added here and will not set cookies either.
